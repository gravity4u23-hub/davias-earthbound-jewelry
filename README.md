## Starting stones

Selenite, tiger's eye, green jade, purple jade, yellow quartz — and any/all natural stone beads. Real science first: quartz is piezoelectric (pressure makes a tiny charge — that's why quartz is in watches). Most stones are insulators, not conductors.
