## 3. The honesty engine — the whole edge

Every claim gets one label before it ever posts:

- **Proven** — real, checkable science (mineral chemistry, hardness, piezoelectricity in quartz).
- **Traditional** — centuries of use, folk and metaphysical claims, labeled as belief and tradition, never as fact.
- **Open** — personal experience, labeled as such ("this is what it feels like to us").

Rules: no invented studies, ever. If a claim can't be sourced, it doesn't go out. No medical or cure claims — that keeps the work honest and keeps the accounts alive.

**One honest flag on the word "conductive":** most of these stones are electrical insulators, not conductors like metal. The real science is narrower and stronger: quartz (tiger's eye, yellow quartz) is **piezoelectric** — it makes a tiny electric charge when squeezed, which is why quartz is in watches. Say that. Don't say the stones conduct electricity like wire, and don't say they heal or cure anything.

## 4. The five starting stones

The line covers any and all crystal stone beads (user, Nov 9). These five are the first profiles — add a new profile the same way (real mineralogy, traditional claims labeled, care notes, what not to claim) whenever a new stone joins the line.

### Selenite
- **Proven:** a clear form of gypsum (calcium sulfate). Very soft — Mohs 2, scratches with a fingernail. The fibrous "selenite" sold in shops is usually satin spar, same mineral, fibrous form.
- **Traditional:** clarity, cleansing a space, calm. Labeled tradition, not fact.
- **Care:** keep dry — gypsum dissolves slowly in water. Wipe clean, don't soak. Handle gently, it chips.
- **Don't claim:** that it cleanses energy in any measured way, or heals anything.

### Tiger's Eye
- **Proven:** quartz, Mohs 7. Its gold-brown bands and shimmer (chatoyancy) come from fine fibers replaced by quartz and iron oxides. Hard and durable, good for everyday bracelets.
- **Traditional:** courage, confidence, protection. Labeled tradition.
- **Care:** tough stone — mild soap and water, fine.
- **Don't claim:** protection or courage as a measured effect.

### Green Jade
- **Proven:** jade is two minerals — nephrite (tough, fibrous) or jadeite (harder, granular). Mohs 6–7. Famous for toughness: it resists breaking better than most stones.
- **Traditional:** harmony, balance, good luck, the heart. Labeled tradition.
- **Care:** durable — mild soap and water, fine.
- **Don't claim:** luck or heart effects as fact.

### Purple Jade
- **Proven:** a purple variety of jadeite, colored by traces of manganese. Same jade family, Mohs 6.5–7.
- **Traditional:** calm, intuition, spirit. Labeled tradition.
- **Care:** durable — mild soap and water, fine.
- **Don't claim:** intuition or spiritual effects as fact.

### Yellow Quartz
- **Proven:** quartz colored golden-yellow by traces of iron — the stone sold as citrine is usually this. Mohs 7, and like all quartz it is **piezoelectric**: pressure makes a small electric charge. That is the real "energy" fact in this whole line.
- **Traditional:** warmth, joy, abundance, clarity. Labeled tradition.
- **Care:** durable — mild soap and water, fine.
- **Don't claim:** abundance or joy as a measured effect.
