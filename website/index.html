Stone guide

Every stone, honestly labeled.

Each claim gets one label. Proven means checkable science. Traditional means belief and history, shared as tradition. Then how to care for it, and what we will never claim.

The real "energy" fact

Quartz is piezoelectric: squeeze it and it makes a tiny electric charge. That is why quartz keeps time in watches. Tiger's eye and yellow quartz are both quartz.

An honest word on "conductive"

Most gemstones are electrical insulators, not conductors like copper wire. We'd rather tell you the true thing, which is more interesting anyway.

Hardness you can feel

Selenite is Mohs 2, soft enough to scratch with a fingernail. Quartz is Mohs 7, harder than a steel knife. That's why care instructions change from stone to stone.
