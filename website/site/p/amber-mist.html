The honest story

What's proven, and what's tradition.

Amber · Proven

Not a mineral: fossilized tree resin, millions of years old. Very soft (Mohs 2 to 2.5) and light. Rub it and it holds a static charge; the Greek word for amber, elektron, gave us "electricity". Plastic and young resin (copal) fakes are common.

Amber · Traditional

Warmth and comfort. Shared as tradition.

Smoky Quartz · Proven

Quartz darkened by natural radiation in the rock acting on traces of aluminum. Mohs 7, piezoelectric like all quartz.

Smoky Quartz · Traditional

Grounding and steadiness. Shared as tradition.

Care

Contains Amber, so the whole piece follows its care: Keep it dry and away from perfume, alcohol, hairspray and heat. Wipe with a soft cloth.
