The honest story

What's proven, and what's tradition.

Black Tourmaline · Proven

Schorl, the iron-rich black tourmaline. A boron silicate, Mohs 7 to 7.5, piezoelectric and pyroelectric like all tourmaline.

Black Tourmaline · Traditional

Protection. Shared as tradition.

Obsidian · Proven

Not a mineral: natural volcanic glass, made when lava cools too fast to form crystals. Mohs 5 to 5.5. It breaks with curved, razor-sharp edges, which is why people made blades from it for thousands of years.

Obsidian · Traditional

Protection and grounding. Shared as tradition.

Care

Durable. Mild soap and water is fine. Mild soap and water is fine. It is glass: a drop on tile can chip it.
