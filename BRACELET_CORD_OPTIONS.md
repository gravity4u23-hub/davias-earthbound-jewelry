# Premium Bracelet Cord Options — Davias Earthbound Jewelry

Owner direction (Nov 17, 2026): Most people use cheap stretchy bands. Find something that feels special, expensive, durable, and long-lasting. Works for country/flag, sports team, beach, holiday, and core crystal pieces.

## Top Recommendations (from 2026 research)

### 1. Nylon-coated beading wire (strongest overall)
- Multi-strand steel core with nylon coating (e.g. 7–49 strand, often called tiger tail or Soft Flex).
- Extremely durable, no stretch, resists fraying.
- Finish with crimp beads + quality clasp (lobster, toggle, or magnetic).
- Best for heavy crystals or daily wear. Looks clean and professional.
- Elevates the piece immediately vs stretch cord.

### 2. Waxed polyester or waxed cotton cord
- Strong, water-resistant, holds knots well, matte or slight sheen finish.
- Great for sliding-knot adjustable bracelets (no metal clasp needed).
- Feels substantial and intentional. Works well with larger beads or as visible design element.
- Excellent for country/flag or sports team pieces where the cord color can match the theme.

### 3. Genuine leather or high-quality faux leather cord
- Round or flat. Soft, durable, improves with age if genuine.
- Best with larger-hole beads or as wrap/macrame style.
- Premium feel. Pair with metal end caps and clasp for a finished look.
- Strong for men’s or unisex sports/country pieces.

### 4. Braided nylon or gel-spun polyethylene thread
- Very strong, low stretch, abrasion resistant.
- Good for knotted designs or when you want the string nearly invisible.
- Higher quality than basic elastic.

### 5. High-quality polyurethane stretch cord (if stretch is still wanted)
- Better than cheap latex or thin elastic (e.g. Beadalon Elasticity or Powercord 0.8–1mm).
- Still not as durable long-term as wire or waxed cord, but a clear upgrade.
- Double the strand for heavier beads + secure knot + flexible glue.

## How to Make It Feel Expensive
- Use quality findings: solid metal clasps, crimps, or end caps (not plastic).
- Consistent bead spacing and professional finishing.
- Matching cord color to the theme (flag colors, team colors, beach neutrals).
- Numbered limited drops with a small certificate or card.
- Photography that shows the construction and materials.

## Recommendation for Davias
- Core and premium drops: nylon-coated beading wire + quality clasp.
- Country, sports, beach, and casual lines: waxed polyester cord with sliding knot or leather cord with end caps.
- Avoid basic thin stretch cord on anything priced above entry level.

All still science-first on the crystals. Cord is a construction/quality upgrade only.
