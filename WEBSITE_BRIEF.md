# Website Brief — daviasearthboundjewelry.love

**Host:** Bluehost.com  
**Domain:** daviasearthboundjewelry.love

## Goal
Upscale, beautiful, professional storefront for handmade-with-love crystal beaded jewelry (bracelets, earrings, necklaces, anklets) with clear room to add more product lines later.

**Feel:** Elegant, calm, high-end but warm and approachable. Easy to navigate and understand. Not cluttered. Not cheap-looking.

## Must-haves
- Clean, spacious product grid / collection pages with large beautiful photos
- Individual product pages with high-quality photos, price, short clear description, and stone education
- Education section for each stone that follows the honesty engine:
  - **Proven** (real mineralogy, hardness, piezoelectricity in quartz, etc.)
  - **Traditional** (clearly labeled as tradition/belief)
  - **Open** (personal experience only)
- About / story page (David & Octavia, handmade with love, science-first mission)
- Contact / order path (DM-to-order or simple form to start; payment method TBD)
- Customizer / recommendation section (user inputs crystals or properties → suggestions)
- Featured monthly limited one-of-a-kind drop section
- Mobile-first, fast, easy to navigate
- Social links (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, etc.)
- Space for future expansion (more stones, new jewelry types)

## Design direction (owner + agents)
- Upscale and professional, not craft-fair casual
- Beautiful typography, generous white space, high-quality imagery
- Logo colors (dark slate, light purple-to-blue gradient, gold accents) used thoughtfully
- Easy to understand for anyone — no jargon without explanation
- Agents add expertise: what would make this feel truly premium and trustworthy

## Content rules
- Science first. Remove woo-woo.
- No medical or cure claims ever.
- Use the existing stone profiles from `davias-master-plan.md` as the starting education content.
- Logo colors and voice stay consistent (warm plain language, honest).

## Platforms to support selling
TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace / Shops, Instagram Shopping, and any others that fit once the site is live.
