# Marketing & Selling Playbook — Davias Earthbound Jewelry

Owner direction (Nov 17, 2026): Research and master marketing/selling tactics. Implement them. Expand into every market: country flag bracelets, sports team bracelets, Christmas/holidays, religious ones. Goal: sell a lot and make real money. Science-first, honesty engine always.

## Proven Tactics to Implement (2026 research)

### Content that works for jewelry
- **Process / behind-the-scenes videos** (Octavia making pieces) — highest organic reach on TikTok and Instagram. Show hands, beads, close-ups of shimmer.
- **Short educational Reels/TikToks** (15–45 sec): one proven fact per stone (piezoelectricity, Mohs hardness, care). Science-first, no woo-woo.
- **Product movement videos**: rotate bracelet under light to show chatoyancy or color. Satisfying content performs well.
- **Mix**: ~50% product, ~30% process/story, ~20% direct sales (drops, limited, restocks).
- Consistency: 3–5 short videos per week minimum. Stories daily.
- Test organically first, then boost only winners.

### Scarcity & drops
- Real limited quantity (start ~100). State the exact number.
- 7-day teaser → countdown → 24–72 hour window → “sold out” proof.
- Numbered pieces or certificates for higher-premium drops.
- Email/SMS list built before the drop (company Gmail). SMS at launch converts highest for time-sensitive offers.
- Early access for email list or previous buyers.

### Platforms priority
1. TikTok + Instagram (discovery + sales)
2. Pinterest (long-term traffic for jewelry)
3. Facebook (shops + groups)
4. Email/SMS (owned audience)

### Selling mechanics
- Clear product pages with large photos, price, and honesty labels.
- Easy order path (DM or simple form to start).
- Packaging that looks premium even if cheap.
- Social proof: customer photos, “sold out” posts, behind-the-scenes.

## New Markets to Hit

### Country / flag bracelets
- Bracelets using crystal colors that match national flag colors.
- High demand exists (Etsy/Amazon show strong sales for flag and soccer fan bracelets).
- Science-first: still use real stones, label any symbolic meaning as Traditional.
- Start with popular countries (USA, Mexico, Brazil, Italy, etc.) and expand.

### Sports team bracelets
- Team color combinations using available crystals + small charms if wanted.
- Game-day and fan gifts. Strong seasonal spikes.
- Custom requests via the site recommendation section.

### Holidays & seasons
- Christmas, Valentine’s, Mother’s Day, etc. — limited colorways or drops timed to the holiday.
- Religious / cultural occasions where color or symbolism fits (still honesty-engine labeled).

### How to expand without losing the brand
- Keep the core science-first voice.
- These are additional product lines, not a change to the mission.
- Use the same packaging, photography style, and honesty labels.
- Monthly drop can sometimes be a themed one (holiday, country, team) once following is built.
