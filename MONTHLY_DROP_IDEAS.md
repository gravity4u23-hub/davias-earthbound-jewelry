# 20 Monthly Limited Drop Concepts — Davias Earthbound Jewelry

Owner direction (Nov 17, 2026):
- Once a month, limited one-of-a-kind style drop (bracelet / earring / anklet / necklace).
- Start small (aim for ~100 pieces) while building following. Scale toward 1,000 only after demand is proven.
- Sell at a premium during a 24–72 hour window.
- Goal: scarcity + people feel special and smart for buying. Science-first, no woo-woo.
- Ultimate goal: sell a lot and make real money.

Each concept below is ready for Octavia to make and for marketing to promote. All follow the honesty engine.

4. **Jade Balance** — Green jade + purple jade mixed. Toughness of jade as the proven fact.

6. **Sunrise Band** — Tiger’s eye + yellow quartz gradient. Warm tones, high-end spacing.
7. **Midnight Jade** — Purple jade only with tiny dark spacers. Calm color story, manganese fact.
8. **Strength Stack** — Green jade + tiger’s eye. Both durable stones, everyday wear angle.
9. **Clear Intent** — Clear quartz + selenite. Minimal, elegant, care notes front and center.
10. **Golden Thread** — Yellow quartz with one thin gold chain accent or charm. Looks expensive.
11. **Forest Path** — Green jade + small natural wood or dark stone spacers. Earthbound theme.
12. **Violet Charge** — Purple jade + yellow quartz. Color contrast that photographs well.
13. **Tiger Night** — Tiger’s eye + purple jade. Bold and unique.
14. **Soft Gold** — Selenite with gold-tone findings only. Delicate, premium packaging focus.
15. **Quartz Core** — Yellow quartz center stone with clear quartz sides. Piezoelectric story.
16. **Jade Garden** — Green and purple jade alternating. Clean, colorful, durable.

18. **Shimmer Line** — Tiger’s eye with chatoyancy highlighted in video. Close-up marketing.

20. **Founders Drop** — One special design by Octavia + David. Numbered 1–100. Highest premium.

## Quantity Guidance
- Start with 50–100 pieces per drop while building audience.
- Only increase toward 500–1,000 once sell-out rate is consistently high.
- Always state exact quantity made (“Only 100 made”).

## Marketing & Content Mission
Focused team effort needed to drive traffic and sell volume:
- Content engine: short science-first videos, product close-ups, drop countdowns, behind-the-scenes of Octavia making pieces.
- Platforms: TikTok + Instagram first, then Facebook and Pinterest.
- Drop marketing: 7-day teaser, 48-hour countdown, live or story updates during the window, post-drop “sold out” proof.
- Email/SMS list building from day one (company Gmail).
- Goal: consistent traffic so each drop sells through and regular products move daily.

All content stays Proven / Traditional / Open. Nothing posts without owner approval.
